## Email 1: Decision Request to Manager

**Subject:** DECISION NEEDED: Senior Engineering Hire - Response by Friday Feb 7

Hi [Manager Name],

I need your decision on adding a Senior Software Engineer to the team by Friday, February 7th.

**Background:**
The team is currently at capacity with missed Q1 deadlines and a 30% decrease in velocity. We have two major client deliverables in Q2 (8 weeks away) that are at risk without additional capacity.

**Options:**

**Option A: Hire Senior Software Engineer (Recommended)**
- Investment: ~$220K first year (~$185K subsequent years)
- Timeline: 8-10 weeks to full productivity
- Pros: Long-term capacity, addresses burnout, reduces technical debt
- Cons: May not fully address Q2 deadlines given timeline

**Option B: Engage Contractor for 6 Months**
- Investment: ~$200-240K for 6 months
- Timeline: Immediate productivity
- Pros: Immediate Q2 relief, flexible engagement
- Cons: Higher long-term cost, no capacity building

**Option C: No Hire - Redistribute Scope**
- Investment: No additional cost
- Timeline: Immediate
- Pros: Budget preservation
- Cons: High Q2 delivery risk, continued team stress

**My Recommendation:** Option A (Hire) with a 4-6 week contractor bridge during onboarding to address Q2 timeline concerns.

**If Approved:**
- Immediate next steps: Finalize job description, begin recruiting
- Budget confirmation: ~$220K FY2025
- Success metrics: Offer accepted by week 6, start date week 8

Full decision brief and stakeholder analysis attached for your review.

Can we schedule a 15-minute call this week to discuss if you have questions?

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Objection Response Quick Reference

### "Budget is tight"
> "Compare $200K investment to $50-100K cost of missed deliveries. One prevented issue pays for 3-6 months. ROI positive within 12 months."

### "Timeline too long for Q2"
> "Valid concern. Contractor bridge provides immediate Q2 relief. Permanent hire addresses long-term. We're doing both."

### "Can we optimize instead?"
> "We've optimized and improved, hitting efficiency limits. Additional workload is beyond what process improvements can address."

### "What if workload decreases?"
> "Roadmap shows sustained demand through Q4. We also have 6+ months of technical debt to address. Capacity will be utilized."
