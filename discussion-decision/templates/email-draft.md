# Email Draft Template

Use this template for structured, actionable communication.

---

## Email Structure

```
Subject: [Action Required] + [Topic] + [Deadline]

[Opening: State purpose and what you need in 1-2 sentences]

[Context: Why this matters now - 2-3 sentences]

[Key Points: 3 bullet points maximum]

[Ask: Clear action request with specific details]

[Next steps: What happens after they respond]

[Contact: How to reach you with questions]
```

---

## Subject Line Formulas

### For Action Requests
- `ACTION REQUIRED: [Decision/Task] - Due [Day/Date]`
- `Approval Needed: [Project Name] - [Brief Description]`

### For Information Sharing
- `UPDATE: [Topic] - [Key Change]`
- `FYI: [Topic] - [Why it matters to them]`

### For Meetings
- `Meeting: [Purpose] - [Date/Time] - [RSVP by]`
- `Invitation: [Topic] Discussion - [Date/Time]`

### For Bad News
- `Important Update: [Topic] - [Action Required]`
- `Change to [Project/Plan] - [Impact]`

- [Point 3: Relevant context]

**Request:**

**What this means for you:**
- [Impact on them: what changes, what they need to do]

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Scenario-Specific Templates

### Decision Request Email

**Subject:** DECISION NEEDED: [Project/Initiative] - Response by [Day/Date]

Hi [Name],

I need your decision on [topic] by [date/time].

**Background:**
We're [current situation] and need to [what needs to happen]. This decision will [impact/consequence].

**Options:**
- **Option A:** [Brief description] - [Key pros/cons]
- **Option B:** [Brief description] - [Key pros/cons]

**My Recommendation:** Option [X] because [1-2 sentence rationale].

**If Approved:**
- [Immediate next step]
- [Timeline for implementation]
- [Who's responsible]

Please confirm if we should proceed with Option [X] or if you'd like to discuss further.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

### Status Update Email

**Subject:** UPDATE: [Project Name] - [Key Milestone/Change]

Hi [Name],

**Quick Update:** [One sentence summary of what's new]

**Progress Since Last Update:**
- [Achievement 1]: [Impact/result]
- [Achievement 2]: [Impact/result]

**Current Status:**
- **Overall:** [On Track / At Risk / Off Track]
- **Key Metrics:** [2-3 important numbers]
- **Upcoming:** [Next milestone or deadline]

**Blockers/Concerns:**
- [Issue 1]: [Status / resolution plan]
- [Issue 2]: [Status / resolution plan]

**What I Need From You:**
- [Specific action if needed, or "Nothing at this time"]

Let me know if you'd like more detail on any of these items.

Best,
[Your Name]

### Bad News Email

**Subject:** Important Update: [Topic] - [Impact]

Hi [Name],

I need to inform you of [situation - deliver bad news directly but professionally].

**What Happened:**
[Clear, factual explanation of the situation]

**Why:**
[Root cause or context - avoid blame, focus on facts]

**Impact:**
- [Impact on them: what changes]
- [Timeline: new dates or delays]
- [Consequences: what this means]

**What We're Doing:**
- [Action 1]: [How we're addressing it]
- [Action 2]: [How we're preventing recurrence]

**What You Need to Do:**
- [Any required actions on their part, or "Nothing required at this time"]

I understand this is [disappointing/frustrating/difficult]. I'm available to discuss this further and answer any questions.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Email Checklist

Before sending, verify:

- [ ] **Subject line** is clear and includes action/deadline
- [ ] **Purpose is stated** in the first 1-2 sentences
- [ ] **Context is clear** - why this matters now
- [ ] **Action request is specific** - what, when, how
- [ ] **Key points are bulleted** - no more than 3-5
- [ ] **Next steps are defined** - what happens next
- [ ] **Tone is appropriate** - professional but not stiff
- [ ] **Length is reasonable** - can be read in under 2 minutes
- [ ] **Recipient is clear** on what they need to do
- [ ] **Contact info** is included for questions

## Common Email Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't bury the lead
- Bad: Starting with background before stating what you need
- Good: State your ask first, then provide context

### Don't be vague about timing
- Bad: "Let me know your thoughts soon"
- Good: "Please confirm by Thursday at 5 PM"

### Don't use unclear subject lines
- Bad: "Update" or "Question"
- Good: "ACTION REQUIRED: Budget Approval - Due Friday"

### Don't make them work to understand
- Bad: Long paragraphs with mixed topics
- Good: Clear sections with headers and bullets

### Don't leave next steps ambiguous
- Bad: "Let's discuss this further"
- Good: "I'll call you on Wednesday at 2 PM to discuss"
