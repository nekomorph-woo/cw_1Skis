## Meeting Type Templates

### Decision Meeting Template

**Purpose:** Make a specific decision

**Agenda Structure:**
1. Context (5 min) - Background on the decision
2. Options Review (10 min) - Present options being considered
3. Discussion (15 min) - Explore pros/cons, concerns
4. Decision (5 min) - Make and record the decision
5. Next Steps (5 min) - What happens next

**Preparation:** Send options document 24h in advance

**Outcome:** Decision clearly stated with rationale

---

### Problem-Solving Meeting Template

**Purpose:** Work through a specific problem

**Agenda Structure:**
1. Problem Definition (5 min) - Clearly define the problem
2. Root Cause Analysis (15 min) - Identify underlying causes
3. Solution Generation (15 min) - Brainstorm potential solutions
4. Selection (10 min) - Choose best approach
5. Action Planning (5 min) - Assign next steps

**Preparation:** Attendees should come with data on the problem

**Outcome:** Problem defined, solution selected, actions assigned

---

### Status Update Meeting Template

**Purpose:** Share progress on a project

**Agenda Structure:**
1. Quick Wins (5 min) - Celebrate progress
2. Status Overview (10 min) - Overall project health
3. Blockers & Risks (15 min) - What's in the way
4. Upcoming Work (5 min) - What's next
5. Questions & Discussion (5 min) - Open floor

**Preparation:** Update status documents before meeting

**Outcome:** Everyone aligned on current state and what's needed

---

### Brainstorming Meeting Template

**Purpose:** Generate ideas on a topic

**Agenda Structure:**
1. Context (5 min) - Set the stage, define scope
2. Idea Generation (20 min) - Rapid brainstorming
3. Grouping & Clarification (10 min) - Organize ideas
4. Selection (10 min) - Vote/prioritize ideas
5. Next Steps (5 min) - Who does what with selected ideas

**Preparation:** Send topic and any constraints in advance

**Outcome:** List of prioritized ideas with owners

## Meeting Facilitation Tips

### Starting the Meeting
- State the purpose and expected outcome
- Confirm agenda and time allocations
- Set any ground rules (e.g., no phones, one speaker at a time)
- Check if everyone has reviewed pre-read materials

### During the Meeting
- Keep time - don't let one topic dominate
- Encourage participation from all attendees
- Park off-topic discussions for later
- Summarize key points before moving to next topic
- Capture decisions and action items in real-time

### Ending the Meeting
- Recap decisions made
- Review action items with owners and due dates
- Confirm next steps
- Confirm follow-up communication
- End on time - respect people's time

### Common Meeting Pitfalls to Avoid

**Don't:**
- Start without a clear purpose
- Let one person dominate
- Go off on tangents
- End without clear action items
- Run over time

**Do:**
- Send agenda in advance
- Start on time
- Keep focus on outcomes
- Document decisions and actions
- End with clear next steps

## Meeting Effectiveness Checklist

**Before the Meeting:**
- [ ] Purpose is clear and specific
- [ ] Agenda is structured with time allocations
- [ ] Only necessary people are invited
- [ ] Pre-read materials are sent 24h in advance
- [ ] Meeting space or platform is ready

**During the Meeting:**
- [ ] Start and end on time
- [ ] Purpose is restated at the beginning
- [ ] Agenda is followed
- [ ] Everyone participates
- [ ] Decisions are recorded as they're made
- [ ] Action items are specific with owners and dates

**After the Meeting:**
- [ ] Meeting summary is sent within 24h
- [ ] Action items are tracked
- [ ] Decisions are communicated to relevant stakeholders
- [ ] Follow-up is scheduled if needed
